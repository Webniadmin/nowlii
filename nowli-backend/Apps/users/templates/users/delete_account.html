Delete your NOWLII account

This page permanently deletes a NOWLII account and its data. You don't need the app to use it.

How it works

  • Enter the email address you use for NOWLII.
  • We email you a 6-digit code to prove the account is yours.
  • Enter the code and confirm. The account is deleted immediately.

You can also do this in the app: Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete My Account.

What is deleted

  • Your account, login details and profile, including your photo
  • Your quests, subtasks, streaks and insights
  • Your AI call records, call summaries and the emotion and mood data from them
  • Your support messages and settings
  • Your subscription, which is cancelled immediately, and your saved payment card

What is kept, and for how long

  • Payment records (invoices and charges) stay with our payment provider, Stripe, for as long as tax and accounting law requires. We no longer hold them.
  • Encrypted database backups may still contain your data until they are overwritten on their rolling schedule, within 35 days.

Can't receive the email?

If you signed in with Apple and chose "Hide My Email", or no longer have access to the address, delete the account from the app, or email hello@nowlii.com with the subject "Delete my account" and how you sign in, and we'll verify it's you and delete it for you.
