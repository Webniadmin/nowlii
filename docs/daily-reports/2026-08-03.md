# 2026-08-03 — the money flow, on a device at last

Eighteen commits. Two days of stranded work committed and deployed, then the paywall was
finally exercised end to end against production instead of being reasoned about.

Three things are worth knowing before the detail.

**The device test finally happened** — on the emulator against live production, not on a
phone, but with real accounts and real 402s rather than mocks.

**Half the settings screen was theatre.** AI Personalization had four controls; one worked.
The worst told the user their data had been deleted and deleted nothing.

**The store cannot sell this plan.** A Google Play offer allows two pricing phases and an
Apple introductory offer allows one. The four-step ladder does not fit either, which reshaped
the whole payments plan.

## Shipped

### The 2026-08-01 backlog, committed and deployed (`de99cf9`…`39cfc19`)

Five days of features were on disk only. Committed as five reviewable pieces — receipts, home

### The price ladder, as the stores can actually carry it (`990d8f5`, `0f68ddb`, `3082c4b`)

Neither store can express a price that steps down four times. So each step is its own store
product, and a subscriber moves down the ladder by a plan change at renewal.

The uncomfortable part, and the reason most of that commit exists: **neither store offers a
server-side plan change.** Only the device can do it. So a subscriber who stops opening the
app keeps paying the older, higher price and nothing tells them. `GET /me/` now carries a
`step_down` block, `POST /confirm-switch/` records what the store did, and
`step_down_pending_since` plus an admin filter make the overpayment findable rather than
silent. It is not a fix — only a device can fix it — but the cost of the design is now visible.

Stages are named from the design: **Spark → Rhythm → Independence → Release → Graduated**,
served from `/plan/` so the app keeps no second list.

### Selling on the web with Stripe, researched and recorded (`837d727`)

Stripe subscription schedules express the phased price natively, which deletes the
overpayment problem instead of monitoring it — and it is *less* work than IAP, not more.
Linking out from the app is now allowed in the US (Apple since 2025-05 at 0%, Google since
2025-12-09 with fees and reporting from **2026-10-01**), plus EU / South Korea / Japan under
their own regimes, and **nowhere else**.

Written up in `subscriptions-iap.md` as an option, not a decision. It is gated on one
business question: **which markets launch first.**

### AI Personalization, made real (`5877bb4`)

Three of four rows did nothing. Restricted Topics saved nowhere. The privacy switch wrote to
SharedPreferences that nothing read. "Clear All AI Memory" reported success and deleted
nothing.

Now the settings live on the account, and the topics are **sent to nowli-ai and folded into
the call persona** — the first thing that changes what the AI says per user.
`POST /profiles/clear-ai-memory/` deletes summaries, emotion and low-mood snapshots and
cached insights, and deliberately keeps the `VoiceCall` rows: they are the daily limit's
ledger, so deleting them would hand out a free refill.

Two deliberate choices in the prompt. Restricted topics describe what the companion must not
*raise*, never what the user may say. And safety is carved out explicitly: if anything
suggests risk of harm the companion responds and stays, because a topic preference must never
become a reason to deflect that.

### Settings honesty pass (`2b10483`, `9cc4342`)

Female is now the default voice on both `Profile` and `NowliiPredefinedOption`, and existing
accounts were moved with it (`users.0018`) — a default change alone applies to nobody who
already exists. nowli-ai already fell back to the female voice, so the stored value now
agrees with what callers actually heard.

Español and "Rate Nowlii" are blurred and inert rather than hidden: hiding an unfinished
feature makes the app look finished and then surprises the user. Four screens pointed at an
asset whose filename contains a `?` — impossible on Windows, so it never existed and rendered
broken everywhere.

### The Pro screen shows a subscriber where they are (`9adc422`, `2f57422`)

The timeline skipped the opening stage, which is right for someone being quoted a price and
wrong for someone paying it: **Spark was missing from its own journey.** Subscribers now see
the whole ladder in four states — COMPLETED, CURRENT PLAN, NEXT PLAN, PROVISIONAL — with the
headline and button changing from a quote to "Ready to continue?" / "Continue for $X".

Found on the way: **Settings → "Nowlii Pro" opened the seven-days-free pitch**, so a paying
subscriber tapping their own plan was offered a trial.

The rail was drawn as five stubs, because each row only drew the segment leaving its dot while
the dot sat 24px down. Rows now draw the arriving segment too, so the colour changes on the
dot — where a stage actually changes.
