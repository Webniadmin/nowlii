# 2026-08-05 — the phone's clock, five screens that disagreed with themselves, and a full deploy

Fourteen commits, all pushed. The backend reached production, and the first APK that can be
put on a phone against the live HTTPS backend was built.

Three things are worth knowing before the detail.

**Every call reminder was firing hours late, and the app said so on its own card.** A quest
set for 11:20 showed "Call scheduled for 13:20" underneath it. The server was reading the
user's wall-clock time in its own timezone, which is UTC.

**Editing a quest never touched its reminder.** Two screens pushed the edit without awaiting
it, so nothing ran on return — not the list reload, not the reminder rebuild. Proven with
`dumpsys`: the armed alarms were the same objects, to the millisecond.

**The subscription screen was selling to people who had already bought.** Opened by a
subscriber on Spark month 1, it read "READY TO CONTINUE?" over a button offering to charge
them $19.99, directly above a row marked CURRENT PLAN.

---

## Shipped

### Reminders now follow the phone

`Quests.select_a_date` / `select_a_time` are naive wall-clock fields. Something has to decide
which clock they are on, and it was `TIME_ZONE = 'UTC'`. A call set for 11:20 in Belgrade was
stored as 11:20 UTC and came back to the phone as 13:20 — every reminder late by the device's
whole offset. Reproduced on the emulator (Europe/Budapest, +02:00) before touching anything:
alarms armed at 12:10 and 13:15 for quests set at 10:15 and 11:20.

The phone reports its **IANA zone** (`Profile.timezone`), sent at signup and again whenever
it changes. An offset would have been wrong by December and wrong the moment a user flew
anywhere; a zone name resolves per date. There is a test for the December case.

`_calls_used_today` moved off the server's day too — a Belgrade user's two sparks were
resetting at 02:00 their time and handing back two hours of the previous evening.

**Correction made mid-session:** the first claim that existing rows could not be fixed was
wrong. The quest still holds the wall-clock time, so the instant can be recomputed. What is
true is narrower: it cannot be a *data migration*, because at deploy time no profile has a
zone yet. It hangs off the profile save instead, and re-derives every pending call for that
user the first time their phone reports in.

### The reminder chain, verified end to end

The delivery itself was never in doubt on paper and never proven. It is now: a notification
landed at 12:12:45 reading `Time to talk to Nowlii` / `"RTazmizsli o kupovini byd" at 12:15
starts in 5 minutes.`, and a second at 12:28:39 for a quest whose time had just been edited —
which is what proves the edit-path fix.

Three faults on that path, all fixed:

- **`sync()` never ran after an edit.** `today.dart` and `scheduled.dart` pushed the edit
  screen without `await`. The project's own rule — call `sync()` after a quest changes — was
  not being kept on the one path where the schedule actually changes.
- **The list showed the old time.** A quest edited to 09:58 went on reading 13:20 until the
  app was restarted, at which point it admitted the call had been at 11:58 and was missed.
- **The edit screen did not know the quest's time.** It reads `taskData['time']`; neither
  caller sent that key, so the picker opened on the current clock. `TimePickerCard` also only
  parsed `"09:58"` while the API sends a Django `TimeField` — `"09:58:00"` failed a length
  check and was discarded in silence.

### A day that cannot take a call stops offering one

Scheduling never reserves quota, so nothing stopped a user planning a third call on a
two-call day, or one for a day whose sparks were spent. Both ended the same way: a reminder
fires, they tap it, the backend refuses. The Enable-call card now blurs with the reason
named, keyed to the day the quest is on rather than to today. And swiping to talk when a call
is planned now offers that call the same time tomorrow instead of leaving it to fail.

### Screens that stopped disagreeing with themselves

- **The month grid put every mark on the wrong weekday.** The backend sends one entry per day
  from the 1st; the grid dropped that straight into a 7-wide layout, so the 1st always landed
  under "Mo". August 2026 starts on a Saturday — every mark was two columns off. Only a month
  beginning on a Monday was ever right. The day number is printed now too: `_buildDayCircle`
  took it and threw it away, so the grid held no date anywhere.
- **The completion banner called every tick the first.** Beside it, "+1 streak" was hardcoded
  and wrong more often — a streak day only lands when every quest on the date is done.
- **The progress bar drew a pill at zero.** The floor that keeps one-of-five from looking like
  a sliver was applied unconditionally.
- **Five zone colour tables, and only Soft steps agreed.** A Power move was red on Quests,
  coral in suggestions, lilac on Soft steps. The badge's *text* colour was a second copy of
  the same problem — two screens derived it from luminance and put white on the orange. And
  the one screen where the user *chooses* a zone showed the same blue whichever they picked.
- **"You can't schedule a quest in the past" with no time picked.** `TimePickerCard` reported
  the clock at the moment the screen opened, then never updated — the timer it declared was
  never started. Typing a title took longer than the one-minute grace.

### Design work

- **Daily streak background** from Figma `15:1933`. The bloom turned out **byte-identical**
  to the `home_flower.svg` already shipped, so it is reused with a colour filter rather than
  committed twice.
- **The out-of-sparks card answers itself.** It looked pressable long before it was; tapping
  now opens the sheet from `1:1000`, ending on the next step the user named on their last
  call — the real `nextStep`, hidden rather than invented when there is none.
- **Most completed quests** now lists only quests finished more than once, which is what the
  sentence above it claims.
- **"Wednesday" fits on one line**, and Settings' first row reads "Your membership" per
  `5:2015`.

### Deploy and build

## Open going into tomorrow

**The trial-ending reminders do not exist.** The "SEVEN DAYS. ON US." screen promises, in its
own timeline, a reminder on day 5 and another on day 6. There is no scheduler, no
notification, no cron, no email — anywhere. The screen makes two commitments the product
cannot keep, and a user's trial ends in silence. This needs a decision: local notifications
(the mechanism already exists and was proven twice today) or a backend job.

**Two zone colours are unconfirmed.** Stretch zone `#3D87F5` and Power move `#D53D40` are the
app's existing values; the design system has no named token and `node-id=0-1` is a page root.
Changing them is now one constant instead of five files.

**The dev machine ran out of disk mid-session** — 0 bytes free, which failed a build and made
`flutter test` return nothing at all. ~2.5 GB was recovered by deleting `%TEMP%\flutter_tools.*`
scratch. It is at 8 GB now, which is still tight; something has eaten 551 GB.

Unchanged: no upload keystore, `activate` is still a mock, Terms of Service still does not
exist. Two test quests were left on the prod account (`Test poziv A`).
