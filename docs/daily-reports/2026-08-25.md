## 1. Notifications and the timezone, settled on a device

The 08-21 work had never run anywhere. It does now, and the mechanism is proved end to end
on the emulator — a quest set for 12:30 rang at **12:30:00.026**.

What the system actually recorded:

```
RTC_WAKEUP  com.nowlii.app
tag=*walarm*:com.nowlii.app/…ScheduledNotificationReceiver
origWhen=2026-08-25 12:30:00.000  window=0  exactAllowReason=permission
```

`window=0` and `exactAllowReason=permission` mean the exact path was taken, not the inexact
fallback. The epoch was `1787653800` = **10:30:00 UTC** = 12:30 in the device's own zone
(`Europe/Budapest`, +02:00). Had the wall-clock time been read as UTC anywhere in the chain,
the alarm would have stood at 14:30 local. It did not.

The chain is clean: the backend stores the bare string `"12:30:00"`, `questAlarmFireTime`
builds a naive local `DateTime`, and `tz.TZDateTime.from(fireAt, tz.local)` resolves it in
the zone `FlutterTimezone` reports. No `toUtc()`, no fixed offset.

**But the permission dialog never appeared on the path a real user takes.** It only appeared
when I created a quest. See §9.

## 2. The quest alarm now arrives five minutes early

A quest alarm fired *at* the quest's own time, which is the one moment a warning is too late
to act on. It now fires `questAlarmLeadMinutes` (5) before.

Three things had to move with it, and each would have been a bug on its own:

- **The notification read the wrong clock.** The body formatted the *fire* time, so simply
  subtracting five minutes would have announced a 12:30 quest as "— 12:25." `questStartTime`
  is now separate from the fire time, and the copy measures the gap rather than assuming it.
- **A quest created inside its own lead window** — "remind me at 12:30", typed at 12:28 —
  would have got **no alarm at all**, because the reminder's moment had already passed. It
  fires immediately instead, which is what a calendar does.
- **"Already passed" is decided by the quest's time, not the reminder's.** Otherwise the lead
  time resurrects quests that have already begun.

## 3. The streak could only ever grow

`GET /api/quests/streak/` counted consecutive days ending at the *latest completed date* and
never compared that date to now. It could rise or hold, never lapse: two finished days last
January still reported a streak of 2 in August.

The rule now: the run must reach **today or yesterday**, or it is 0. Yesterday counts because
today's quests may simply not be finished yet, and ending a streak at midday punishes a day
that is still running.

Two corrections came with it:

- **The day is decided on the user's calendar** (`user_localdate`). `select_a_date` is a naive
  wall-clock date, so asking a UTC server which day it is gives the wrong answer to anyone
  east of Greenwich for the first hours of their day. The same rule has now bitten call
  reminders, the spark reset, quest suggestions and this.
- **Future dates are excluded.** A quest can be created *and ticked* for tomorrow; without
  this, finishing tomorrow's quest today awarded two days at once.

`Apps/quests/tests.py` is **the first test this backend has ever had** — 11 cases, including
the January-in-August one. Note that `manage.py test Apps.quests` crashes (`Apps/` has no
`__init__.py`); the working label is `Apps.quests.tests`.

The flame on the home screen was wired correctly the whole time. The endpoint was wrong.

## 4. Activity trend was drawing every bar at a twentieth of its height

`maxY` was hardcoded to `20`. Nobody finishes twenty quests in a day, so every real bar drew
at 1/20 of the space available and the chart read as empty — which is what "does activity
trend work?" was about. The axis follows the week now, floored at 4 so a quiet week does not
stretch one quest to full height, and rounded up to a multiple of four so the gridline labels
stay whole numbers.

The day mapping was fine: the backend anchors the week with
`monday = ref - timedelta(days=ref.weekday())`, so Mon–Sun line up.

## 5. One calendar instead of two that disagreed

Insights and My Progress each owned a calendar. The week strip on My Progress was a row of
two hand-picked icons — a blue tick or a sun — with its own day labels and **no skipped state
at all**, so a day you missed and a day still ahead looked identical. (That also explains the
"all seven circles ticked under 0-Day Streak" note that had been sitting unexplained in the
checklist.)

Both are `widget/quest_calendar.dart` now: a month is five rows of it, a week is one. Three
states from Figma `53:10201` — `#FAE3CE` with an orange check, `#DFEFFF` with a blue minus,
`#F5F5F5` empty. The two glyphs are the **exported SVGs**, which carry their own fill
(`#FF8F26`, `#89B6F8`), so they render untinted at their designed 20 inside the designed 40.5
circle.

**The backend's `streak` status folds into `consistent`.** The design has no third mark, and
a streak day *is* a day where everything got done. The old grid's day numbers go with it —
the design has none.

Verified against real data: August 2026 starts on a Saturday and the grid puts the 1st under
**Sa**; a skipped Friday and a consistent Tuesday both render correctly. That also answers
"does Insights compute skipped/consistent?" — it does.

Two things worth knowing about the calendar's own logic, which was already correct: the
`streak` mark only appears after **seven** consecutive full days (different from the flame),
and it is computed only from quests inside the queried range, so a run crossing a month
boundary is truncated.

## 6. The quest card is the target now

The checkbox completes the quest; anywhere else on the card opens it. The checkbox keeps its
own `GestureDetector` and wins the tap outright — Flutter's gesture arena resolves in favour
of the innermost recogniser — so ticking never opens the editor. Verified both ways on the
device.

The edit pencil is retired. Once the card is the target, a second control for the same action
only competes with the checkbox opposite it. Same treatment on the Scheduled card, which has
no checkbox at all (there is nothing to tick on a day that has not arrived).

## 7. The suggested cards were navy text on a navy plate

"5 mins" replaces "10 mins", the Shuffle button is gone (suggestions still refresh on every
load, which is all the button did), and the moon matches the add button at 48 instead of 64.

The white-title request turned out to be wider than it looked. `moon4` is the night art **and
the fallback for every task matching no keyword**, so *most* suggestion cards were dark navy
text on a dark navy background, not just "Meditation session". Rather than hardcoding white
for one string, the card picks its ink from its background: title, description and the
date/time row. The two pills keep their navy text — they carry their own light fill and were
always readable.

## 8. The empty state was fixed once and in one file

`today.dart` got the fix on 08-14. `scheduled`, `completed` and `blocking` kept
`SizedBox(height: screenHeight - 200)`. Those 200 are roughly the header and tabs on the

## 9. Nothing runs after login

This is the one to carry forward. `sign_in_screen.dart` (lines 157 and 193, both the email and
Google paths) goes straight to `context.go('/homeScreen')`. The complete set of `sync()`
triggers is: creating a quest, ticking one, editing one, moving a call to tomorrow, the end of
a call, and a **cold start where a token already exists** (`splash.dart:100`).

There is no "after login" call site, though `CLAUDE.md` says there should be. Two consequences,
both seen live:

- The notification permission is never requested until the user creates their first quest.
  `POST_NOTIFICATIONS granted=false` the whole way through sign-in and the home screen.
- **`DeviceTimezone.report()` is on the same chain**, so a user signing in on a phone in a new
  zone does not report it until the next cold start. Quest alarms are local and unaffected;
  this hits `ScheduledCall`, which the backend derives from `Profile.timezone`.

Not fixed today — it wants a decision about where the call belongs.

## 10. Profile, receipts, settings

**The edit icon was a bug, not a bad choice of art.** The row drew
`Image.asset(..., color: iconColor)`, and `color:` flattens *every pixel* to one value.
`Edit profilIcon.png` is a blue plate with a navy pencil on it, so painting it white produced
a **solid white disc with no pencil in it**. The row takes a widget now and the pencil is an
outline, matching the "i" below it.

Also on the profile: no ring around the photo or the heart, the name pill in `#A0E871` and cut
from 265×74 to 200×56 (most of the screen's width for a five-letter name), and **"Talk to
Fuzzy"** — the *tenth* place addressing the companion by a name the user never chose, far
enough down the screen that nobody had scrolled to it. The other nine went on 08-21.

**Active quests** counted every quest ever left unticked, so the number climbed forever and
never fell. A quest whose day came and went unfinished is *missed*, not active.

**Receipts** got a back button (the screen had no way out but the system gesture) and
swipe-to-delete. That needed a backend endpoint: **`DELETE /api/voice-calls/<id>/summary/`**
did not exist. Keyed by the *call* id because `CallSummary` has no id of its own on the wire.
The call survives the delete, so a receipt cannot be used to buy back a spark. Removal is
optimistic and the receipt is put back if the server refuses.

**Settings:** the membership row wears the mark from Figma `3368:29245`.

## 11. Two things that only look like features

- **All five notification toggles are dead.** Settings → Notifications writes
  `notif_task_reminders` and four siblings to SharedPreferences and **nothing reads them** —
  grep confirms the only reader is the screen itself, drawing its own switches. Turning "Task
  Reminders" off does nothing. Three of the five categories ("Daily Tips", "Nowlii Check-ins",
  "AI Insights & Reflections") have no sender anywhere in the app.
- **The in-app notification feed on the profile is permanently empty.** The design
  (`53:10668`) shows five populated cards; nothing writes that feed. The code says as much:
  "the design's populated list is aspirational".

The app posts exactly **two** kinds of system notification: quest alarms
(`nowlii_quest_alarms`) and call reminders (`nowlii_call_reminders`), both from
`call_reminder_service.dart`.
