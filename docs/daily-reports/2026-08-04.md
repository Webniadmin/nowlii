# 2026-08-04 — a lapsed plan stops evicting people, and four screens stop lying

The backend that had been sitting on disk went to production, the paywall stopped refusing
the people it exists to convert, and a day of testing as a real user turned up four separate
places where the app reported success it had not achieved.

Three things are worth knowing before the detail.

**The account was the instrument.** Both QA allowlists were emptied, so `pavle` now hits the
real trial, the real paywall and the real two-calls-a-day limit. Half of today's findings were
invisible until that happened.

**A lapse no longer closes the app.** Reads survive, writes get 402, and a dialog says so.
That decision came from the client mid-session and reshaped the gate.

**Four "successes" were fictional.** Changing the companion, choosing one at signup, the
picture on the naming screen, and the Power-moves ring each reported or drew something that
was not true.

## Shipped

### Yesterday's backend, deployed

`users.0016/0017/0018` and the nowli-ai personalization changes reached production. Verified
inside the running container rather than from the deploy log — `showmigrations` marks `[X]`,
and `POST /profiles/clear-ai-memory/` answers **401 rather than 404**, which is the
credential-free proof the route exists.

Restricted Topics then survived a force-stop and relaunch on the emulator, so they are coming
back from the account and not from a local cache.

Runbook correction: `-f docker-compose.prod.yml` is **required** on the box. A bare
`docker compose build` in `~/backend` fails with "no configuration file provided".

### The paywall could not take money from anyone on a trial

`GET /subscriptions/me/` returns `subscribed: true` for **anyone with a Subscription row** —
which includes every user still inside the free trial. The Pro screen read that as "this
person already pays" and rendered a permanently disabled CTA. So a trial user opening
Settings → Nowlii Pro saw a screen quoting them a schedule with a dead button underneath.

`_isSubscribed` now agrees with the rest of the screen and keys off `month_index`, which is 0
until the paid schedule starts.

### Partial lock: a paused account, not a confiscated one

New `HasProAccessOrReadOnly` — safe methods pass, writes raise 402. Applied to quests,
subtasks, insights, rest-days and the receipt library. Strict `HasProAccess` stays on
everything that costs money per call: voice-call start/end/quota, scheduled calls, AI subtask
generation.

Insights serves the user's own numbers after a lapse but **skips the AI generation**, falling
back to the data-derived copy built from those same numbers. The statistics are theirs; the
paragraphs written over them are the paid part.

The router guard went from an allowlist of four permitted screens to a denylist of the paid
ones. A once-per-launch dialog carries the news, with a plain "Not now".

Three traps showed up only on the device:

- **`splash.dart` still hard-redirected to the paywall on launch**, silently defeating the
  entire router change.
- **The quota endpoint's 402 was read as "quota unknown"**, and unknown is deliberately
  silent — so the home card sat on "Checking your sparks…" forever and still offered a call.
  Hence `SparkState.paused()`, distinct from `.unknown()`.
- **"See you tomorrow" was false.** Nothing returns on its own after a lapse.

### Four screens that reported success without it

- **Changing the companion did nothing.** The picker sent `avatar_logo` (a URL) and
  `nowlii_name`, both **read-only** server-side — the companion is set by `predefined_option`.
  The request returned 200, the server discarded both fields, and the screen announced
  "Avatar updated successfully!". Proven fixed on the device: request `{predefined_option: 3}`,
  response `avatar_logo: knotty.png`.
- **The same defect at signup.** Onboarding had `option.id` in hand and stored only the URL;
  `createProfile` had no parameter for it at all. **Every new account got the default
  companion** — and with it the wrong AI voice, since `Profile.save()` adopts the companion's
  voice.
- **The naming screen showed the wrong character.** It picked the picture by matching the
  companion's *name* against the catalogue. Rename your companion — which that screen invites
  — and nothing matches, so it fell back to the first entry. It also hard-coded
  `avatarOptions[0]` whenever the name was custom. `ProfileModel` was not even parsing
  `predefined_option`; now it does, and the picture follows the id.
- **The Power-moves ring drew full for any completion** (`powerMoves > 0 ? 1.0 : 0.0`). One
  of five looked like five of five.

### "Your moves" was dynamic, and still unreadable

It read real per-zone data but showed only the *completed* count. A week with two unfinished
quests rendered "0", which is indistinguishable from having no quests at all — exactly the
doubt it was reported under. Each ring now carries its denominator ("of 4" / "none set").

### Design work

- **Spark is the opening row of the paywall timeline**, badged **NEXT** in green, with the
  rest unchanged. Given its own status rather than reusing `next`, whose grey outline is
  correct for a subscriber's future stage and wrong for the thing being sold. `confirmedSteps`
  is now counted from row 1, or adding the row would have quietly demoted Independence to
  PROVISIONAL.
- **The profile plan card names the plan**: FREE TRIAL with days left, `SPARK PLAN` with price
  and month, FREE FOREVER, or the expired variants. "Nowlii Pro" survives only as the loading
  state. Stage names come from `/plan/`, so the app holds no second list.
- **The profile card gained "N quests active"** and the orange expired state with Renew.
- **Home restored to the original design** (Figma `1:1317`): the blue "Ready to make today
  count?" card with "Todays progress", and the quest list back as orange rows. The sparks box
  stays at the top and the swipe button is unchanged; the green closing card takes the hero
  slot once the day's sparks are spent.

## Verified

- 169 backend tests, 180 Flutter tests, `flutter analyze` 0 errors
- Lapsed state exercised end to end against production: launch lands on home, Progress and
  the profile load, Add quest redirects to the paywall, the swipe CTA works there
- Trial countdown checked at day 4 ("4 days left") and day 10 (lapsed)
- The mock purchase moves the account to `SPARK PLAN · $19.99/mo · month 1`

Four old tests asserted the full-lock behaviour and were rewritten; five asserted the paywall
timeline started at Rhythm. Three more proved the kill switch and allowlist using a **GET** on
`/api/quests/` — now open to everyone, so they would have passed even if the bypass were
broken. Moved to writes.
