### 2026-09-22: account deletion, second AAB
- Public page **`https://api.nowlii.com/delete-account/`** (`7e39249`), which Play's Data
  safety form requires. It emails a 6-digit code: HMAC-stored, single use, 5 attempts,
  1 mail/min. It gives the same answer whether or not the address has an account.
- **A deleted account kept being charged.** Deletion never touched Stripe. Both paths
  (the app and the page) now delete the Stripe customer first, and they refuse to delete
  the account if Stripe fails. Checked against the Stripe test API. Deployed with
